I am a first-year Masters student pursueing a Masters in Robotics at the Univeristy of Pennsylvania, affiliated with GRASP Labs.

I am interested in robot control, robotic manipulators, and embedded systems. My focus includes control systems, robotics design, machine learning applications, and embedded systems programming.

I have an BS in Mechanical Engineering as well as a minor in Computer Science from The Univeristy of Tennessee, Knoxville, where I was a research assistant for Dr. Caleb Rucker as part of REACH Robotics Lab as well as a research assistant for Dr. Kwai Wong as a part of NICS
